# NextRole – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 7, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

NextRole – Resume Builder (“NextRole,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is a resume-building application that helps users create, edit, import, and export professional resumes.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using NextRole.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on the information you enter or import, resumes may contain:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and mailing addresses
- Employment history, education, skills, and certifications
- Professional summaries and other resume content
- Additional personal information voluntarily included in resumes

You control the information you include in your resumes.

## 3. Resume Storage

NextRole stores resumes created within the application locally on your device.

When you use the resume-import feature, resume information is transmitted to our backend service for processing.

## 4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

NextRole uses OpenAI technology to help extract and organize information from imported resumes.

When you import a supported document, its contents may be transmitted to our backend and OpenAI for processing.

Do not include sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your resume.

## 5. How We Use Information

We process resume information to:

- Create and edit resumes
- Import and organize resume information
- Provide AI-assisted document processing
- Generate resumes for export
- Maintain the functionality and security of the application

We do not use resume information for targeted advertising or sell resume information to data brokers.

## 6. Third-Party Services

NextRole uses third-party infrastructure and services, including:

- **OpenAI:** AI-assisted resume processing
- **Render:** Backend hosting and request processing

These providers may process information according to their applicable service terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Data Retention

Resumes saved locally remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application's local data.

Imported resume information is processed through our backend and AI service. Third-party service providers may retain certain information under their applicable policies and operational requirements.

## 8. Data Security

We use HTTPS to transmit information to our backend. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 9. Your Choices

You may edit or delete locally saved resumes using the application's available features.

You may also avoid using AI-assisted importing and create resumes manually.

## 10. Children's Privacy

NextRole is intended for general resume-building and career-related use. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services, or data practices change. Updates will be reflected by the date at the top of this page.
